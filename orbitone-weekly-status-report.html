04

OrbitOne Reminder lead gen campaign

Status: ready to launch

The full campaign strategy is complete. Ad copies, targeting, lead form structure, KPI benchmarks and creative briefs are documented and reviewed. Launch is queued and will execute the moment the video creative is received from your team. No additional setup time is required on our end.

Campaign structure Built by Antonio
  • Campaign: OrbitOne Reminder Lead Gen AU. Objective: Leads via Meta Instant Form.
  • Ad Set: AU Xero business users. Budget: $8.33 AUD/day ($250 over 30 days).
  • Ad 1: Pain point angle. "Still chasing unpaid invoices?" Targets emotional exhaustion of manual follow-up.
  • Ad 2: Feature-led angle. Speaks directly to Xero users. Highlights multi-channel automation.
  • Ad 3: Outcome-led angle. Focuses on time saved and cash flow improvement. Recommended as video format.
Lead form and targeting Built by Antonio
  • Four-field form: full name, business email, business name, and Xero qualifier question.
  • Interest targeting: Xero, online accounting, invoicing, business productivity, small business management.
  • Behavioural layer: small business owners, active business pages, B2B decision makers.
  • Demographic: age 25 to 55, all Australian states, English language.
  • Exclusions: existing website visitors and current customer email list.
KPI targets 30-day window
  • Cost per lead: under $15 AUD. Conservative estimate: 15 to 17 leads total. Optimistic: 22 to 25 leads.
  • Lead form completion rate: 15% or above.
  • Link click-through rate: 1.5% to 2.5%.
  • Video view rate (ThruPlay, 3 seconds): 25% or above.
  • Reach target: 8,000 to 15,000 Australian business accounts over 30 days.
